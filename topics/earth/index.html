🗿 What Really Happened on Easter Island

The Jared Diamond ecocide story — a civilization cutting down its own trees and starving itself — is a hell of a parable and mostly wrong. Three independent lines of evidence converged on Rapa Nui in the last two years: shortwave infrared satellite imagery and machine learning mapping every lithic-mulch rock garden on the island, pointing to a population ceiling nearer 3,000 than 15,000 (a finding that is genuinely contested, and flagged as such); 800 years of rainfall read out of hydrogen isotopes in lake sediment, showing a real drought the Rapanui adapted to rather than collapsed under; and a conductivity meter walked along the coastline, which found fresh water at every single ahu. The actual collapse has a date and a cause, and it is not the Rapanui.
