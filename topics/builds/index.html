🍺 HopLove

An open hop-variety database that stores every source's number instead of one confident one: Hopsteiner, BarthHaas, the Hop Breeding Company, Indie Hops and more, rolled up with the disagreements left visible. 232 varieties, a hop landscape chart, hop-vs-hop comparisons, Washington, Oregon and Idaho acreage by variety from the USDA National Hop Report, and What's in the Can: 2,000+ beers from 32 breweries from Bellingham to Corvallis, hop by hop, Scan a Beer, which reads the hops off a photo of the can, and Untappd check-ins and ratings joined to the hops. Free JSON API.
