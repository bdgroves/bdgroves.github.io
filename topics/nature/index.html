🐟 Survivors of an Inland Sea

Pyramid Lake's native fish are survivors of Lake Lahontan, an ice-age lake bigger than Lake Ontario, mapped here by flooding today's terrain to its 1,335 m shoreline. The cui-ui lives nowhere else; the Lahontan cutthroat that made the 41-pound world record vanished after Derby Dam and came back from Pilot Peak, confirmed by DNA from a UNR lab. GBIF maps of five natives, the dams on the Truckee, and heat shock proteins from the author's UNR days.

🐟 Two Creeks of Gold

California's state fish, the golden trout, evolved in just two creeks on the Kern Plateau. In 1876 a coffee can carried 13 of them over the Sierra crest; now GBIF has 1,133 records across 13 states and provinces, only 288 of them on the plateau and 333 in Idaho. Plus the 1904 warning to Theodore Roosevelt, hybrids, brown trout and cattle, meadow streams that hit 26 °C, and an open question about heat shock proteins.

🪨 What’s Up With the Pikas?

The American pika is the poster animal of climate change in the mountains, and the science is split between alarm and calm. I checked 276 of its pre-1950 museum sites against every GBIF record since 2000. Where pikas are still found, they're found where they always were; the sites missing modern records are disproportionately the low ones for their latitude, matching what the field resurveys found.

🦎 Six Islands, Six Lizards

On Guadeloupe one anole lineage stranded on six islands became six species: the Marie-Galante giant, the golden-eyed anole of Petite Terre, the Kahouanne anole whose entire range is two rocks. 🌫️ Where the Fog Lives

The Namib runs on fog, and a 2026 study found bacteria growing inside fog droplets and eating formaldehyde. If fog is a habitat it needs a habitat map: FOG-WATCH builds one from ten years of ERA5 reanalysis via Open-Meteo, for the Namib, the Atacama and the California coast. The Namib map fades inland and peaks at dawn from October to February, matching a new ground-based cloud-base study almost exactly. Plus the fog-basking beetle that does the headstand, and the one that got the credit.
