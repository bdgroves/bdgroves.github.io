# Illustration prompts: the diorama posts

The "dream version" picture for each diorama post: the place as a little
hand-made model on a desk. It goes in the post after the first paragraph,
with the caption "The dream version, as an AI illustration..." (Seattle,
Oct 2026, was the first). Save the result as `blog/img/<post>/illustration.jpg`
or send it to Claude to wire in.

## Seattle (done, for reference)

```
Create a wide, warm, photorealistic illustration of a hand-made miniature Seattle sitting on a wooden desk at sunset, like a tilt-shift photograph of a beautiful scale model.

THE MODEL:
- The Space Needle rising from Seattle Center, surrounded by autumn trees in orange, red, gold and green.
- The red Monorail train on its curving concrete beam down 5th Avenue.
- Two round, ribbed hotel towers side by side (the Westin Seattle) and three glass geodesic spheres among downtown towers.
- Elliott Bay along one edge, with a green-and-white ferry and its V-shaped wake.
- Mount Rainier glowing pink and white far beyond, as if the window behind the desk opens onto it.

THE DESK:
Warm late light from a window, a vintage camera and a folded paper map beside the model, soft depth of field, the model's edges showing it is a crafted object with a cut base of land and water.

MOOD:
Cozy, joyful, nostalgic, the feeling of a place you love made small enough to hold. Golden hour light, rich warm colours with cool blue water.

CONSTRAINTS:
- No words, labels, logos, signs, numbers or watermarks.
- No people's faces.
- 16:9, wide.
- Generate the image directly without asking follow-up questions.
```

## Yosemite

```

THE MODEL:
- The valley seen from its west end, as from Tunnel View: El Capitan's great granite wall on the left, Bridalveil Fall and Cathedral Rocks on the right, and Half Dome at the far end.
- Yosemite Falls pouring white down the north wall in spring flow.
- The Merced River winding through meadows and pine forest on the valley floor.
- A tiny cluster of colourful tents under the pines below the north wall (Camp 4), and a few minuscule points of light on El Capitan's face where climbers are bivouacked.
- The base of the model cut away to show layers of granite, with the high country and a hint of Tuolumne's domes rising beyond.

THE DESK:
Warm late light from a window, a well-worn climbing guidebook (no readable text), a coil of rope, a coffee mug and a folded paper map beside the model, soft depth of field, so it reads clearly as a crafted object.

MOOD:
Cozy, joyful, nostalgic, a Sierra kid's home ground made small enough to hold. Warm alpenglow on the granite, deep green forest, cool blue shadows in the valley.

CONSTRAINTS:
- No words, labels, logos, signs, numbers or watermarks.
- No people's faces.
- 16:9, wide.
- Generate the image directly without asking follow-up questions.
```

## Amalfi Coast

```
Create a wide, warm, photorealistic illustration of a hand-made miniature Amalfi Coast sitting on a wooden table on a sunny terrace, like a tilt-shift photograph of a beautiful scale model.

THE MODEL:
- The steep green Sorrento peninsula rising out of a deep blue sea, its cliffs dropping straight into the water.
- Positano's pastel houses stacked up the hillside above a beach of tiny striped umbrellas, with a white ferry and its wake leaving the pier.
- The coast road winding along the cliffs with a few tiny cars, a small blue bus and a cyclist.
- Ravello's gardens on a green ridge high above Amalfi's cathedral and harbour.
- The island of Capri off the tip, with its limestone sea stacks (the Faraglioni) and small boats at anchor; a few tiny hidden coves with swimmers.

THE TABLE:
Bright Mediterranean afternoon light, a lemon tree branch and a few lemons, an Aperol spritz and a cold bottle of limoncello beer beside the model, a folded paper map, soft depth of field, so it reads clearly as a crafted object with a cut base of rock and sea.

MOOD:
Sunny, joyful, wistful, a favourite trip made small enough to hold and dream about going back to. Lemon yellow, bougainvillea pink, terracotta and cream against deep Tyrrhenian blue.

CONSTRAINTS:
- No words, labels, logos, signs, numbers or watermarks (including on the drinks).
- No people's faces.
- 16:9, wide.
- Generate the image directly without asking follow-up questions.
```

## Zermatt & the Matterhorn

```
Create a wide, warm, photorealistic illustration of a hand-made miniature Zermatt and the Matterhorn sitting on a wooden table by a window on a crisp summer morning, like a tilt-shift photograph of a beautiful scale model.

THE MODEL:
- The Matterhorn rising sharp and pointed at the back of the model, its Hörnli ridge running toward the viewer, rock faces streaked with snow, a pink glow of first light on the summit.
- The village of Zermatt in the valley below: dark larch-wood chalets on white stone bases with grey slate roofs and bright red geraniums in the window boxes, a little church spire, and a river running through.
- Red gondola cabins strung along a cable from the village up the green slopes, with tiny pylons, and a big red cable car crossing toward a glacier.
- The little red Gornergrat cog train climbing through larch woods and past a small lake that reflects the Matterhorn.
- Green alpine pastures with a herd of black Eringer and brown cows with bells, two or three of them dressed for the Alpabzug in tall crowns of colorful flowers with big brass bells on embroidered straps.
- An alphorn player in a red vest, white shirt and black hat on a grassy shoulder, his long wooden alphorn resting on the meadow, pointed toward the Matterhorn.
- A couple of colorful paragliders drifting over the valley, a small Swiss flag on a hut, and white glaciers spilling off the peaks on one side.
- The base of the model cut away to show layers of rock.

THE TABLE:
Soft morning window light, a mug of hot chocolate, a small wedge of Swiss cheese and a folded hiking map beside the model, soft depth of field, so it reads clearly as a crafted object.

MOOD:
Joyful, storybook, crisp and clean: the Switzerland of postcards made small enough to hold. Deep greens, warm wood browns, geranium red, snow white and a clear blue sky.

CONSTRAINTS:
- No words, labels, logos, signs, numbers or watermarks.
- No people's faces in detail; figures are tiny.
- 16:9, wide.
- Generate the image directly without asking follow-up questions.
```
